Add Bar Management Portal that enables bar attendants to efficiently manage inventory, process sales, track daily transactions, and generate receipts. The system should support multiple shifts, maintain accurate stock records, and provide detailed sales reporting.

After login as a bar attendant of any branch, the Bar Attendant should see:

Total drinks in stock.
Low-stock alerts.
Today's sales summary.
Total bottles sold.
Revenue generated.
Current shift status (Open/Closed).
Recent transactions.

Drink Inventory Management

The system should allow the Super Admin to:

Add new drinks.
Edit drink details.
Delete drinks.
Categorize drinks (Beer, Wine, Spirit, Soft Drinks, Water, Energy Drinks, etc.).
Set selling price.
Record available quantity.
Track stock movement.
Receive notifications when stock is low.
View stock history.

Each drink should include:

Drink Name
Category
Brand
Selling Price
Quantity Available
Barcode (Optional)
Image (Optional)
Status (Available/Out of Stock)

Sales Module

The Bar Attendant should be able to:

Select the waiter or waitress from a list or enter their name.
Add multiple drinks to a single order.
Specify:
Drink name
Quantity
Unit price
Automatically calculate:
Item subtotal
Grand total
Automatically deduct sold quantities from inventory.
Save every transaction.

Each sale should generate a unique invoice number.

Receipt Generation

After completing a sale, the system should generate a professional receipt containing:

Company Name
Company Logo
Invoice Number
Date & Time
Waiter/Waitress Name
Drinks Sold
Quantity
Unit Price
Total Amount
Bar Attendant's Name
Payment Method
QR Code (Optional)

The receipt should support:

Printing to a POS thermal printer.
Downloading as PDF.
Reprinting previous receipts.
Emailing receipts (Optional).

Waiter/Waitress Sales History

Each waiter or waitress should have:

Purchase history.
Total drinks collected.
Outstanding balance (if credit is allowed).
Daily transaction records.

Search by:

Name
Date
Invoice Number

Shift Management

The system must support multiple work shifts.

The Bar Attendant should be able to:

Open a shift.
View current shift sales.
Close the shift at the end of duty.

When a shift is closed:

Calculate total revenue.
Calculate total drinks sold.
Record opening and closing stock.
Generate a Shift Summary Report.
Lock the shift to prevent further editing.
Allow the next attendant to start a new shift with a fresh session.

Daily Sales Reports

Generate reports by:

Today
Yesterday
Weekly
Monthly
Custom Date Range

Reports should include:

Total Revenue
Number of Transactions
Drinks Sold
Top-Selling Drinks
Low Stock Items
Sales by Waiter/Waitress
Sales by Bar Attendant

Reports should be exportable to:

PDF
Excel
CSV

Notifications

Notify users when:

Stock is running low.
A shift has been closed.
Inventory is updated.
Daily sales target has been achieved.

Search & Filters

Enable searches by:

Drink Name
Waiter/Waitress
Invoice Number
Date
Category
Bar Attendant

POS & Printing

Support:

USB POS thermal printers.
Bluetooth POS printers.
Network printers.
Standard A4 printers.

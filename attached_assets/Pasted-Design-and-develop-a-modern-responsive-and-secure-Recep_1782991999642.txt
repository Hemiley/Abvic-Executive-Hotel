Design and develop a modern, responsive, and secure Receptionist Dashboard for a luxury hotel management system. The dashboard should enable receptionists to efficiently manage guest check-ins, room bookings, reservations, payments, and shift operations. The interface should follow 2027 UI/UX standards, featuring a clean design, glassmorphism, smooth animations, and an intuitive workflow.

Create a secure login system specifically for receptionists.

Features:

Receptionist Login

Role-Based Access

Shift Management System

Every receptionist must operate under an individual shift.

Shift Start

When a receptionist logs in:

Start Shift
Record Login Time
Record Receptionist Name
Record Opening Balance (Cash Drawer)
Mark Shift as Active
Shift Operations

During the shift, the system should automatically record:

Walk-in Bookings
Reservations Processed
Room Changes
Guest Check-ins
Guest Check-outs
Payments Received
Refunds Issued
Discounts Applied
Additional Services Sold
Total Revenue
Cash Transactions
Card Transactions
Bank Transfers

Every transaction should be linked to the receptionist currently on duty.

Shift Closing (After 24 Hours)

At the end of the shift (or when the receptionist finishes duty), the system should require the receptionist to Close Shift.

The system should automatically generate a Shift Summary containing:

Receptionist Name
Shift Date
Login Time
Logout Time
Total Working Hours
Number of Guests Served
Number of Rooms Booked
Reservations Processed
Total Sales
Cash Sales
Card Sales
Transfer Payments
Discounts Given
Refunds
Closing Balance
Cash Variance (if any)

The receptionist must review and confirm the report before closing the shift.

Shift Handover

Once the shift is closed:

Lock the previous shift
Prevent further edits
Archive all shift records
Generate a printable Shift Report
Allow the next receptionist to log in and start a new shift
Ensure each receptionist can only view and manage transactions recorded during their own active shift, while supervisors and administrators have access to all historical records.

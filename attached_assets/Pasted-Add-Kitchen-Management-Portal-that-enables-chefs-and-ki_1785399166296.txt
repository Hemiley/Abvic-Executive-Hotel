Add Kitchen Management Portal that enables chefs and kitchen staff to efficiently manage kitchen operations, inventory, food preparation, and order fulfillment. The system should be secure, and support multiple shifts.

Authentication & Shift Management

* Secure login for Chef
* Role-based access control.
* Allow chefs to **Start Shift** and **End Shift**.
* Record:

  * Staff name
  * Login time
  * Logout time
  * Shift duration
  * Opening stock
  * Closing stock
  * Shift notes
* Prevent new staff from starting a shift until the previous shift has been properly closed or handed over.

Kitchen Inventory Management

Allow chefs and the super admin to record, monitor, and manage all kitchen inventory, including but not limited to:

* Chicken
* Turkey
* Fish
* Beef
* Goat meat
* Pork (optional)
* Shrimp
* Rice
* Vegetables
* Cooking oil
* Seasonings
* Drinks used in the kitchen
* Frozen foods
* Other ingredients

Each inventory item should include:

* Item Name
* Category
* Unit of Measurement (kg, liters, cartons, pieces, packs, etc.)
* Price per items
* Opening Stock
* New Stock Received
* Current Stock
* Minimum Stock Level
* Supplier
* Purchase Cost
* Expiry Date (where applicable)
* Last Updated Date
* Status (Available, Low Stock, Out of Stock)

Stock Control and prices
allow super admin to:
* Add new stock
* Add new price and update existing prices
* Update existing stock
* Deduct stock automatically when meals are prepared
* Track stock movement history
* Notify when stock reaches minimum quantity
* Display low-stock alerts on the dashboard

Kitchen Order Management

The kitchen should receive food orders in real time from:

* Waiters
* Waitresses
* Receptionists
* Bar staff (Barman/Bar Lady) for food requests

Each order should display:

* Order Number
* Table Number or Room Number
* Customer Name (optional)
* Order Source (Restaurant, Bar, Room Service, Reception)
* Staff Name who placed the order
* Food Items Ordered
* Quantity
* Special Instructions
* Time Order Was Placed
* Priority (Normal, Urgent, VIP)

### Order Workflow

Each order should move through the following statuses:

* New Order
* Accepted
* Preparing
* Ready for Pickup
* Served
* Cancelled

Kitchen staff should be able to:

* Accept orders
* Reject unavailable items
* Estimate preparation time
* Mark orders as ready
* Notify the waiter or receptionist when the order is complete

### Kitchen Dashboard

Display real-time statistics including:

* Active Orders
* Pending Orders
* Completed Orders
* Cancelled Orders
* Total Meals Prepared Today
* Current Kitchen Staff on Duty
* Low Stock Items
* Out-of-Stock Items
* Today's Ingredient Usage

### Reports

Generate reports for:

* Daily Kitchen Production
* Daily Ingredient Usage
* Weekly and Monthly Stock Consumption
* Waste Report
* Spoilage Report
* Shift Performance
* Most Ordered Meals
* Kitchen Revenue Summary (if applicable)

Reports should be exportable as:

* PDF
* Excel
* CSV

### Notifications

Real-time notifications for:

* New food orders
* Low stock alerts
* Out-of-stock items
* Shift reminders
* Delayed orders
* Completed orders ready for pickup

### Receipt & Kitchen Tickets

Generate printable kitchen order tickets containing:

* Order Number
* Table/Room Number
* Ordered Items
* Quantity
* Special Instructions
* Date & Time
* Staff Name

Support:

* POP Printer Printing
* PDF Download

### Audit Trail

Record every activity, including:

* Stock added
* Stock removed
* Order accepted
* Order completed
* Shift started
* Shift closed
* Inventory edits

Include:

* User
* Date
* Time
* Action performed


All modules should share the same database so inventory, orders, sales, and reports remain synchronized across the entire hotel management system.
